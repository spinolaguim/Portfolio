My presets for Lightroom

Soft texture, brightness emerging between the lights, and slightly shifted colors. Install them on your phone and bring my style to your own photos.

How to install on Lightroom mobile

Follow the 6 steps below. Works with photos and files already on your phone.

  1. Step 1 photo
    1

    Download the presets to your phone

    Tap the download button at the bottom of this page. If the file comes zipped (.zip), extract it first, because Lightroom cannot open compressed files.

  2. Step 2 photo
    2

    Open a photo in Lightroom

    In the Lightroom app, tap any photo in your library to open it in the editing view.

  3. Step 3 photo
    3

    Open Presets

    In the toolbar, tap Presets.

  4. Step 4 photo
    4

    Import the file

    Open the options menu (the three dots) and tap Import Presets.

  5. Step 5 photo
    5

    Choose the file

    Find the presets file you downloaded, select it and confirm.

  6. Step 6 photo
    6

    Apply it to your photo

    Your presets appear under Yours. Tap one to apply it and adjust the intensity as you like.

Download the presets

The download link will be available here soon. Once downloaded, go back to the steps above to install them.
